About this calculator

What is a BMI calculator?

A BMI calculator estimates Body Mass Index using weight and height. BMI is commonly used as a screening measure that places adult weight relative to height into broad categories.

How to use the BMI calculator

  1. Select a unit system. Choose Metric or Imperial.
  2. Enter your measurements. Use a current height and weight.
  3. Calculate. Press Calculate BMI to see the result.
  4. Interpret carefully. BMI is a screening tool and not a complete measure of health.

How is BMI calculated?

For metric measurements, BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. Imperial measurements use pounds and inches with the standard conversion factor.

BMI categories

Common adult screening ranges are underweight below 18.5, healthy range from 18.5 to 24.9, overweight from 25 to 29.9, and obesity at 30 or above.

Important limitations

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat or account for fat distribution. Interpretation can differ for children, teenagers, pregnancy and some athletic or clinical populations.
