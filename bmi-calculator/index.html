What is BMI?

Body Mass Index, commonly called BMI, is a number calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a screening measure for adults and can help place a person's weight relative to height into broad categories.

How to use this BMI calculator

Select metric or imperial units, enter your height and weight, and select Calculate BMI. The result displays the calculated BMI and the corresponding broad category used by this calculator.
